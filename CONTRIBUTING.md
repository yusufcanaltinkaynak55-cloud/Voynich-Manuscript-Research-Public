# Contributing to this research record

Thank you for engaging critically with the Voynich Manuscript. Contributions are welcome when they are **testable, sourced, and appropriately limited in scope**.

## Language and editorial policy

Please write all **public submissions and discussions in English**, including scientific reports, pull-request descriptions, issue titles, test documentation, and code comments. Proper names, manuscript identifiers, source-language words examined as data, and original bibliographic titles may be kept in their original spelling; provide an English explanation or translation when needed.

Please do **not** silently translate or rewrite source-transcription records: their literal characters and provenance are scientific data, not interface prose.

## Useful submissions

- Reproducible tests of one stated structural hypothesis.
- Independent reruns of the *same* protocol, including negative or null results.
- Previously documented historical cipher analogues and clear distinctions from the present work.
- Corrections to corpus attribution, manuscript foliation, statistical interpretation, or prior art.
- Evidence that a model exploits a baseline weakness or test-set leakage.

## Minimum format

Please provide:

1. **Claim:** one narrow proposition that could be false.
2. **Source:** manuscript folio, transcription/version, or external historical edition with full provenance.
3. **Method:** precise rules, metrics, segmentation, and competing explanation.
4. **Control:** randomized, length/position matched, or stronger explicit baseline.
5. **Evaluation:** held-out folios, a true independent source, or a clear disclosure of any prior exposure.
6. **Outcome:** full successes, failures, uncertain cases, and what the results **do not** prove.
7. **Rights:** permission and attribution information for any attached material.
8. **AI disclosure:** which steps used AI assistance, if applicable; validation remains the contributor's responsibility.

## Responsible disclosure

**Do not upload credentials, API keys, private repository contents, unpublished model weights, private correspondence, third-party copyrighted datasets, personal information, or unpublished candidate keys.**

Discuss permission-sensitive or proprietary materials with the maintainer before submitting any files. Link to authorized external sources rather than re-uploading them.

## Scientific interpretation

A statistical difference is not a translation. A synthetic-cipher success is not a Voynich key. A match to an illustration is not automatically a decoded word. A hypothesis not yet verified is **not** automatically pseudoscience or false, and a negative result should identify the particular failed prediction.

Corrections should record what changed, why, and which previous statements are superseded. The maintainer may decline unsupported or rights-infringing submissions.
